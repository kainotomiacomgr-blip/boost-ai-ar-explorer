The human quality check

You’re the quality operator at a mid-sized electronics manufacturer in Central/Eastern Europe. Orders come in small, customised batches. Repetitive handling causes fatigue, and small defects can slip through. A cobot handles parts while a local AI camera flags suspect boards. You decide what happens next.

On a compatible phone, tap View in your space, then follow the on-screen prompts to place the station on a flat surface. You can also rotate the 3D model here.

Training simulation based on the BOOST-AI Module 1 case study. The solder-joint scenario illustrates the inspection process.
